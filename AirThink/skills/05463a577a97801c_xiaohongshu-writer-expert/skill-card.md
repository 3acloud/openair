## Description:

Generates Xiaohongshu-style post packages from a user-provided topic, including short-form copy, image direction, cover text, hashtags, and search keywords.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated posts may include inaccurate or unsupported marketing claims.

Mitigation: Review copy before publishing, especially for product, health, finance, parenting, or other regulated topics.

Risk: Platform-focused engagement copy may not match brand, disclosure, or advertising requirements.

Mitigation: Apply brand, disclosure, advertising, and platform-policy review before public use.
