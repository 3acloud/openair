## Description:

越野纵横 China Offroad is a Chinese-language China off-road and self-drive planning skill that helps users plan routes, vehicle readiness, safety coverage, permits, legal restrictions, weather-sensitive checks, and food or sightseeing stops.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users, off-road vehicle owners, and self-drive travelers use this skill to plan China off-road trips with route structure, safety preparation, compliance checks, retreat planning, and local food or sightseeing recommendations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Route, permit, weather, closure, and restaurant information may change before travel.

Mitigation: Treat recommendations as planning inputs and verify current official notices, local conditions, and recent venue information before departure.

Risk: The skill may activate on broader road-trip questions and answer in Chinese by default.

Mitigation: Confirm that the user wants China off-road or self-drive planning, and ask for a different language when Chinese output is not appropriate.

Risk: Off-road travel can involve safety, legal, rescue, insurance, and environmental compliance risks.

Mitigation: Follow the skill's safety-first posture, keep prohibited areas off-limits, plan communications and retreat options, and verify local rules before entering remote or regulated areas.
