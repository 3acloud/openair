## Description:

Generates photography-grade portrait image prompts and image-generation guidance using posing, lighting, optical-parameter, film-style, reference-image, and anti-artifact checks.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[neuer111](https://clawhub.ai/user/neuer111)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External creators and developers use this skill to generate portrait prompts, parameter summaries, negative prompts, and image-generation guidance for mainstream AI image platforms. It supports quick keyword-based portraits, detailed professional prompt analysis, and reference-image workflows that reproduce pose, lighting, style, and composition when permitted.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reference-image mode can reproduce pose, lighting, style, composition, and sometimes facial identity from uploaded images.

Mitigation: Use only reference images that the user has the right and consent to use, and avoid identity replication without permission.
