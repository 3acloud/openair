## Description:

Generates original clay-style Chinese Zodiac sticker-pack prompts and local asset-processing commands for WeChat sticker submissions, social posts, group-chat stickers, and holiday greeting images.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

Creators and developers use this skill to plan original Chinese Zodiac sticker sets, assemble bilingual image-generation prompts, add captions, and package generated assets for WeChat-style sticker release workflows.

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The included watermark-removal workflow could be used to hide provider, ownership, or provenance marks before publication.

Mitigation: Use the watermark-removal utility only on images you created or are licensed to modify, and avoid removing third-party, platform, attribution, copyright, or provenance marks.

Risk: Generated sticker images may still contain intellectual-property, platform-compliance, or quality issues.

Mitigation: Manually review generated images and packaging outputs before publication, using the skill's originality, content-safety, and WeChat submission checklists.
